Company overview

More focus on the work. Less time chasing supplies.

Comeiin Works (Pty) Ltd is a South African supplier and distribution partner focused on laboratory equipment, consumables and related scientific products. We combine product sourcing, responsive service and practical procurement support.

Founded in 2016 and formally incorporated in 2020, we are built around reliability, quality, competitive pricing and long-term business relationships.

Equipment and consumables for routine operations, research, diagnostics, quality control and industrial applications. Availability is subject to supplier ranges and customer specifications.

  • Consumables and single-use products

Portfolio

  • Laboratory EquipmentCentrifuges, vortex mixers, shakers, rockers, rollers, pipettors, pipette controllers, bottle-top dispensers.
  • Pipetting & Liquid HandlingPipette and filter tips, serological pipettes, liquid-handling accessories.
  • Sample Collection & HandlingBlood collection products, specimen containers, swabs, specimen bags.
  • Laboratory PlasticwareCentrifuge and PCR tubes, cryovials, Petri dishes, racks, reservoirs.
