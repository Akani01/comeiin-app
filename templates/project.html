ESR testingBlood collection & diagnostics

Sanguilon ESR

A completely closed erythrocyte sedimentation rate (ESR) test that protects laboratory personnel from the risks of handling blood. The two-part Westergren system pairs a 220 mm crystal polystyrene pipette, graduated in 1 mm steps over 150 mm, with a polypropylene reservoir tube prefilled with 200 µl of 3.8% sodium citrate. The pipette pierces the stopper, auto-zeroes the blood and sends any excess into the sealed reservoir.
